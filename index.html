Hours are day-level only and were taken in September 2026. "Open today" means the doors open at some point today, not that it's open right now — check the listing before you drive.

Date night, groups, veg-friendly and fine dining are my judgement from price, room type and review language, not data fields.

Coverage follows my searches, not reality. Every venue here came from a keyword search, so a place is missing whenever I didn't search its suburb or its kind of food. Two things get missed: suburbs I never named, and depth anywhere — each search returned its top handful and I moved on, so even Northbridge and Scarborough were missing places with hundreds of reviews. 58 of the 144 suburbs still have two entries or fewer. Perth has roughly 4,000 to 5,000 food businesses, so treat this as a browsable collection rather than a directory. If somewhere obvious is absent, it's a gap in my searching, not a verdict.
